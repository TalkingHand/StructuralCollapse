title=[42.22.2] Structural Collapse
description=[h1]Structural Collapse[/h1]
description=In vanilla Build 42 you can sledgehammer every wall of a ground floor and the upper floors keep floating in the air. With this mod, when nothing is left holding an upper floor up, that floor and everything above it [b]come down one level[/b], keeping their shape.
description=
description=[h2]What holds a floor up[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Walls (door and window frames, garage and truck doors included), pillars and stairs on the level below
description=[*]An upper floor, its walls and its pillars count as one structure: [b]one support left anywhere under it holds the whole thing[/b]. Only a structure with nothing at all under it comes down.
description=[*]Roofs: a roof with no wall, floor, ceiling or roof under it drops one level too
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]When it comes down[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]The floor that lands stays, the one below breaks and drops its materials
description=[*]Furniture that would overlap moves to the nearest free tile (not through walls); with no room it breaks, dropping its contents and materials
description=[*]Players under it are hit on the head (helmets help), zombies under it are killed (on the tough setting about a third survive, knocked down), animals are hurt, vehicles lose condition
description=[*]Corpses and items fall with it; a car parked on an upper floor falls when the floor goes
description=[*]Big floors come down over several ticks, nearest to the removed support first
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Other things that fall[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Free-standing pieces (street light heads, sign tops, billboards, tanks, lattice towers) fall when what they stand on is removed; pieces of one object fall together
description=[*]Hanging objects (ceiling lights, signs on a balcony edge) fall when what they hang on goes
description=[*]Sledging a wooden utility pole breaks the wires hanging on it (you get some electric wire). A high-voltage pylon stands while any of its legs is left; when the last leg goes, the tower falls and its lines break
description=[*]Dismantling one tile of an upper floor drops what stood on it, and a part cut off from every support comes down
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Lua only, no vanilla files replaced. Runs on the server side (works in single player). Checks happen only when something is removed, so it costs nothing while you just play. Safe to add to an existing save: vanilla buildings only move when you remove their supports. A collapse cut off by saving finishes after loading.
description=Multiplayer: not tested.
description=
description=[h2]한국어[/h2]
description=1층 벽·기둥·계단을 모두 부수면 그 위층(과 그 위 모든 층)이 모양 그대로 한 층 내려앉습니다. 위층 바닥·벽·기둥은 한 구조물로 보고, 아래에 지지대가 하나라도 남아 있으면 버팁니다. 겹치는 가구는 가까운 빈칸으로 밀려나고 자리가 없으면 부서져 내용물과 재료를 떨어뜨립니다. 깔린 플레이어는 머리를 다치고(헬멧이 막아 줌), 좀비는 죽고(튼튼함 설정에서는 약 3분의 1이 넘어진 채 버팀), 차량은 내구도가 깎입니다. 지붕, 가로등 머리·간판·광고판 같은 홀로 선 조각, 천장에 매달린 물건도 받치던 것이 없어지면 떨어집니다. 나무 전신주를 부수면 걸린 전선이 끊기고(전선 아이템), 송전탑은 다리가 하나라도 남으면 버티다가 마지막 다리가 없어지면 탑과 선이 무너집니다. 오브젝트를 지울 때만 판정해서 평소에는 부담이 없고, 기존 세이브에 넣어도 됩니다. 멀티는 확인하지 않았습니다.
